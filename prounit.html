Our Production Process

A step-by-step overview of how we refine, purify, and package our water.

Step 01

Water Collection

Raw water is ethically sourced from deep, pristine underground aquifers.

Step 02

Pre-Filtration

Initial filtering stages capture large suspended solids and sediment particles.

Step 03

Sand Filtration

Multi-media sand beds filter out finer turbidity and physical impurities.

Step 04

Activated Carbon

Removes chlorine, unpleasant odours, organic compounds, and corrects taste.

Step 05

Reverse Osmosis

High-pressure semi-permeable membranes strip out dissolved minerals and toxins.

Step 06

UV Sterilization

Ultraviolet lamps eradicate micro-organisms without chemical additives.

Step 07

Ozonation

Infuses active oxygen to eliminate remaining bacteria and extend shelf stability.

Step 08

Automatic Filling

Automated non-contact lines fill bottles and sachets under sterile conditions.

Step 09

Sealing & Packaging

Tamper-proof hermetic sealing locks in ultimate freshness.

Step 10

Quality Inspection

Batch samples undergo rigorous physical, chemical, and microbiological checks.

Step 11

Distribution

Safely dispatched through our fleet to vendors, businesses, and homes.
